Our Heritage

The 4 Steps of Purity

We preserve the ancient Indian tradition of hand-rolling, ensuring zero chemical compression and maximum aroma retention.

1. Sourcing

Selecting only pure botanical resins, gums, and woods.

2. Kneading

Blending ingredients into a fragrant, chemical-free dough.

3. Hand-Rolling

Artisans skillfully roll the dough onto natural bamboo splints.

4. Sun Curing

Slowly air and sun-dried to lock in the deep, resonant aromas.
