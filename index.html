Our Craftsmanship

40+ Years of Indian Artisanal Preservation

Based in the coastal town of Pondicherry, a historic hub of trade and spirituality, Mothers Commerce preserves the ancient tradition of natural incense-making. We reject synthetic chemicals, toxic charcoal cores, and chemical fragrance binders.

Our incense starts with pure sandalwood powder, raw resins, essential oils, and sun-dried flower petals. They are hand-kneaded into dough and rolled onto single bamboo splints by skilled local artisans who have carried this trade for generations.
